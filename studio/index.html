THE STUDIO

Independent in spirit.
Open to every world.

RetroSoul Games is an independent game studio. We’re starting with one game, Cedar Shadows: Hana, and a belief that atmosphere and emotion belong alongside action.

We draw on the spirit of classic games while exploring what modern tools make possible. Our identity isn’t tied to one place or genre. From quiet forests to distant frontiers, we want each world to have its own voice — and every game to feel like it has a soul.
