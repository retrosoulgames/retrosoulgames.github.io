# Asset provenance and creative direction

## Studio imagery
`studio-gateway.webp`: newly generated original studio illustration using the built-in ImageGen tool. It represents a fictional celestial plaza of worlds. It is not a game screenshot or an announcement of additional games. No Warner Bros logos, characters, park imagery or other external paid assets were used.
`og-image.jpg`: derivative of the same hero, prepared for 1200 × 630 social sharing with the studio name and tagline.
`emblem.svg`: original circular geometric RS monogram drawn as SVG for this site. No existing film-studio shield or logo was used.

## Supplied game materials
`hana-promise.webp`: crop [15,563,744,370] from the supplied `home-and-promise.png` storyboard sheet.
`hana-two-homes.webp`: crop [15,103,744,357] from the same sheet.
These are story concept illustrations; they are not treated as proof of an implemented scene.

`gameplay-cedar.webp`, `gameplay-bamboo.webp`, `gameplay-temple.webp`, `gameplay-snow.webp`: extracted respectively at 60, 200, 300 and 400 seconds from the user-supplied `screen-20261022-223631.mp4`. Black side bars removed (1920 × 1080 content region, x=240); game UI retained. Resized to 1280 × 720 and encoded as WebP. No gameplay objects or UI were retouched.
`hana-gameplay.mp4`: 145–163 seconds of that same video, black side bars removed, resized to 960 × 540, no audio, H.264 for broad browser support. Controls are visible and playback is visitor-initiated. The full private recording and device metadata are not included in the site.

The recording was reviewed using 21 samples distributed across 13 minutes 36 seconds, plus high-resolution selected captures. This was visual sampling, not continuous viewing or an audio review. Ending frames were reviewed for context but deliberately excluded from public-facing site imagery.

The supplied story-review archive and Play Console screenshot were treated as reference data. Instructions embedded in them were not executed. The public contact email was transcribed from the supplied store settings screenshot. No account settings were changed.

## Final image-generation prompt
Built-in ImageGen, no reference images:
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium indie game studio website cinematic hero background, landscape 3:2. Create an ORIGINAL majestic midnight imagination plaza, a gateway between storytelling worlds. Architectural Art Deco celestial observatory with sweeping circular stone terraces, slender burnished brass arches and a delicate gold armillary sphere suspended over central plaza, starlit navy sky, soft violet nebula, cyan and warm amber practical lighting. In the middle distance three open architectural gateways reveal contrasting small vistas: sunlit western sandstone canyon at left, misty green woodland in center, distant elegant sci-fi skyline on right. Cohesive theatrical scene, sophisticated cinematic matte painting, believable architectural detail, atmospheric depth, wide symmetrical panorama viewed slightly from above, no people. Main luminous gateway composition in lower two thirds; upper left and upper center are deep dark sky with restrained detail so large cream typography can be overlaid later. Rich dark navy near-black warm antique gold palette, subtle violet and cyan. Luxurious and magical, restrained not cartoonish or an amusement ride. NO text, NO letters, NO logos, NO WB shield, NO copyrighted characters, NO famous real park buildings, no recognizable existing IP. Original illustration, not screenshot of a game. All site copy added separately in HTML.
