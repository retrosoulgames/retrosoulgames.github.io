This policy explains information used by the RetroSoul Games website and its optional Google Analytics measurement.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

About this policy

This policy covers the studio website at retrosoulgames.github.io. The Android game Cedar Shadows: Hana has its own game privacy policy. Website analytics choices do not change the game's advertising or privacy settings.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google, to understand which pages people visit and which links help them discover our work. Analytics is off until you choose Accept analytics. Choosing Reject analytics lets you use the complete website without optional analytics. Ignoring the banner also leaves analytics off.

After you accept, Google may process information such as page titles and addresses, referring websites, visit times, session and cookie identifiers, browser and device characteristics, language, approximate location and interactions such as outbound link clicks. Google receives network information, including your IP address, when your browser contacts its service. Analytics uses pseudonymous cookie and session identifiers and may involve personal data.

We remove query strings and fragments from the initial page address and referring address supplied in our Google tag configuration. Outbound link measurement includes the destination URL, including the game package ID in our Google Play link. Do not put personal or confidential information in website URLs. There are no account-registration or feedback forms on this site.

Our website tag keeps advertising consent denied and disables Google Signals and advertising personalization. These settings apply to this website only.

Cookies and your saved choice

  • Consent preference: your browser's local storage holds rsg.analytics-consent.v1, containing your choice and its date, for up to 180 days. This allows the site to remember acceptance or rejection. It contains no analytics identifier and is not sent to Google by our consent code.
  • Analytics cookies: after acceptance, Google Analytics can set first-party cookies beginning with rsg_ga to distinguish visits and sessions. Our tag sets a maximum lifetime of 180 days, which may be refreshed on later visits while consent remains valid.

If browser storage is blocked, your choice applies only to the current document and you may be asked again on the next page.

Change or withdraw consent

Use Analytics settings in any page's footer to review your choice. Select Reject analytics to stop future collection. If analytics is already running, the page reloads to unload it and the website's analytics cookies are cleared. Changes are also applied to other open tabs. Your saved choice expires after 180 days, when the site asks again.

Withdrawing consent does not erase information previously received by Google. To ask about that information or your applicable privacy rights, contact us using the address below. You can also clear this site's cookies and local storage in your browser to reset your choice. If JavaScript is disabled, this website does not load analytics.

Hosting, service providers and retention

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information needed to serve and protect the website, independently of our optional analytics. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Google processes analytics data under its applicable terms and privacy policies; processing may take place outside your country. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Analytics data retention depends on the settings of our Google Analytics property and Google's applicable policies; it is separate from the browser-storage lifetimes above.

Contact

For questions about website analytics, privacy choices or applicable data rights, email retrosoulgames.dev@gmail.com. If you email us, we use the information you choose to send to respond to your enquiry.
